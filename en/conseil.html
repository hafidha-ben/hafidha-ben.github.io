Engagement formats

Three formats depending on your starting point and the level of involvement needed.

Format 01

Strategic diagnostic

An objective view on a strategic choice, a process losing momentum or a fundraising file to strengthen.

  • Analysis of the situation, options and risks
  • Fundraising file audit and due diligence preparation
  • Summary note and actionable recommendations
Short format · Fast decision
Format 02

Growth & transformation framing

Structuring a market access strategy, a roadmap or a target operating model.

  • Feasibility and market access study (legal, commercial, financial, customer journey)
  • Target operating model and initiative prioritization
  • Rollout plan, adoption and success metrics
Dedicated mandate · Roadmap and deliverables
Format 03

Strategic Advisory

A regular sounding board for executives, founders or committees to keep decisions and execution aligned.

  • Ongoing review of priorities, operating models and trade-offs
  • Critical review of plans, offers or partnerships, including AI-related
  • Advisory board or selection jury participation
Monthly or ad-hoc cadence
What I do not do

To keep roles clear and protect advisory quality, this scope excludes:

  • Software engineering or technical integration of AI solutions.
  • Turnkey technical automation delivery or day-to-day operational management of a rollout.
  • Financial intermediation or success-fee fundraising advisory.
