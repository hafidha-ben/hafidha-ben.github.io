Pre-sales process optimization

CAC 40 enterprise transformation program

Context
A lengthy, siloed deal-validation process with redundant reviews that needed streamlining to respond faster to clients.
Contribution
Target process design (three-track opportunity categorization, clarified roles and governance, unified reviews), digitalization framing (CRM adaptation), rollout and change management.
Outcome
Target process presented to the group’s executive board (June 2023): roughly 96% of opportunities, out of ~158,000 annually, routed to a streamlined track validated by a 120+ deal pilot. Rollout scope: 170,000+ users covered by the design and deployment of the target pre-sales process.
